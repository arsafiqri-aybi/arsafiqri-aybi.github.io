Context & Contribution

Why this work exists.

Executing a command does not establish that a task is complete. Browser state, connection state, and observations must agree, while the owner needs a clear way to take control.

Hey connects an Android application and an MCP gateway to a personal browser. The project focuses on a clear relationship between a request, the observed result, and the device owner's control.

My contribution

Product concept, interaction design, and development with AI assistance.

Project status

In Development · 2026

Decisions & Process

Making system behavior observable.

One browser. Two modes of control.

Use one browser shared between the owner and the agent, report state from a common source, and check action outcomes before calling them successful. Keep connection, browser readiness, and human takeover visible.

Outcome & Limitations

What the evidence can support.

Application and gateway source, Android builds, and test records are available. Device logs document scoped navigation and takeover tests, alongside visual, audio, and service-lifecycle failures that need fixes and retesting.

Current scope

In Development · 2026

What remains limited

The project remains in development. Version 0.2.1 service recovery needs physical-device retesting; scrolling within the browser during human takeover has a reported defect. FCM-based wake is not configured.
