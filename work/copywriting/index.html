Context & Contribution

Why this work exists.

Persuasive wording can hide assumptions. Applying a formula without context can make a message less relevant or introduce unsupported claims.

Copywriting Intelligence starts with real context: the audience, the value offered, objections, and available evidence. Copy patterns are choices rather than mandatory formulas.
