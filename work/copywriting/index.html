01The idea

Clear messages. Grounded claims.

Copywriting Intelligence starts with real context: the audience, the value offered, objections, and available evidence. Copy patterns are choices rather than mandatory formulas.

Persuasive wording can hide assumptions. Applying a formula without context can make a message less relevant or introduce unsupported claims.

02Decisions & contribution

The thinking behind the system.

Connect market context, communication mechanisms, claim boundaries, and critique. Preserve contradictions and uncertainty so research does not become a guarantee of results.

  • Organized seven knowledge cores with context and evidence layers.
  • Designed reasoning, message development, critique, and feedback workflows.
  • Prepared evaluation and records of claim and release boundaries.
03Outcome & limitations

What is available. What remains open.

Release v1.0.0 provides a runtime skill, knowledge modules, a source register, atomic claims, and evaluation within its stated repository scope.

Release evaluation was created and assessed by the building agent, rather than an independent evaluator. It does not establish conversion gains, installation across hosts, or complete scientific coverage.
