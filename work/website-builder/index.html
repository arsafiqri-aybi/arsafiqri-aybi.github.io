01The idea

Design and engineering meet in the experience.

Website Builder places user needs and output quality alongside the code. Presentation, behavior, and verification are designed together.

A website can look attractive yet fail to explain its purpose, be difficult to use, or load too slowly. A successful build does not establish the quality of the experience.

02Decisions & contribution

The thinking behind the system.

Connect goals, friction, design hypotheses, behavior, and verification. Apply perception principles in context, then express them through composition, interaction states, and testable fallbacks.

  • Connected UX, visual identity, frontend development, accessibility, and performance.
  • Organized domain references and evidence-based acceptance gates.
  • Linked the method to Portfolio Web for static frontend implementation.
03Outcome & limitations

What is available. What remains open.

Runtime guidance and references are available in the repository. Portfolio Web complements them with 32 modules, playbooks, examples, and a verification matrix for portfolio websites.

Guidance does not certify the quality of every site that uses it. Psychology-related choices need context; accessibility, performance, and deployment must be checked in the actual implementation.
