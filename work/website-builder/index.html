Context & Contribution

Why this work exists.

A website can look attractive yet fail to explain its purpose, be difficult to use, or load too slowly. A successful build does not establish the quality of the experience.

Website Builder places user needs and output quality alongside the code. Presentation, behavior, and verification are designed together.
