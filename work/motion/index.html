Context & Contribution

Why this work exists.

Choosing an animation library does not explain how movement conveys information. Timing, space, state, and output quality need to work together.

Two connected libraries support the study: Motion covers principles of movement through code, while Motion Render Video focuses on the visual output a viewer actually experiences.

Knowledge architecture and demonstration development with AI assistance.

Outcome & Limitations

What the evidence can support.

Motion documents 40 domains and application paths. Motion Render Video covers 22 core areas, with actual rendered examples including a six-second 2D study and a CPU-rendered 3D demonstration.

What remains limited

The demonstrations test specific mechanisms. They do not establish mastery or validation of every domain. Numerical and CPU-render tests do not establish browser or GPU behavior, viewer comfort, or complete accessibility.
