01The idea

Complex work. A clear direction.

Scale and Governor separate two decisions that are often mixed together: how work is organized and how resources are used during execution.

Long tasks can lose their goal, repeat work, or report completion before verification. Small tasks can also accumulate orchestration they do not need.

02Decisions & contribution

The thinking behind the system.

Scale defines dependencies, outputs, verification, and adaptation boundaries. Governor works within that contract, adjusting context, retrieval, and strategy, and requesting replanning when the boundary is reached.

  • Defined the responsibilities and adaptation boundaries of both systems.
  • Developed execution contracts, failure guidance, and handoff procedures.
  • Included validators and static audits for structural consistency.
03Outcome & limitations

What is available. What remains open.

Both repositories provide runtime skills, references, example contracts, validators, and evaluation records. Work structure and result status can be recorded explicitly.

Static validation does not establish automatic invocation, model switching, or cost savings across hosts. Benefits in real work need comparable measurement.
