Overview

Exploring a clearer order journey.

A collaborative academic project translating customer research into high-fidelity designs for exploring hampers, reviewing orders, and continuing to WhatsApp for confirmation.

Design scope

From exploring to confirming.

The public project summary describes a high-fidelity UX flow: browse hampers, review an order and continue the conversation on WhatsApp. Detailed research and decision records have not yet been published.
